### 🧠 About me

AI Engineer and Data Scientist based in the Netherlands 🇳🇱. I build **AI agents and assistants that work on real documents and real systems**: legal research, SEO + GEO, education, internal company workflows. Underneath the LLM work sits a classic **ML engineering and data science** foundation: forecasting, gradient boosting, deep learning and production ML pipelines.

My rule: **measure first.** No change without a baseline score, and every fix shows its before/after.
